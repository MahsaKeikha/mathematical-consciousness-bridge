# Figure preparation contract

Before submission export:

- preserve vector originals whenever the journal accepts the format;
- ensure every figure is readable at journal column width;
- replace repository-specific navigation text with manuscript-facing labels if necessary, without changing mathematical content;
- keep exact fractions visible where they are scientifically relevant;
- identify generated or synthetic content directly in the caption;
- define every abbreviation used inside the graphic;
- use the same notation as the manuscript;
- include the proposition source in the internal provenance record, but do not require a journal reader to understand repository numbering to interpret the figure;
- do not use decorative imagery as a substitute for a theorem or quantitative visualization.
