Reject the complete declared family

P71: When is a successful bridge test non-circular?

A target derived from the same descriptor being tested can satisfy the bridge by construction. P71 therefore requires scientifically independent target provenance before a successful factorization can count as bridge evidence. Read P71.

P72: What survives noisy target measurement?

Under the declared nondifferential measurement condition, target noise can attenuate or erase a real latent witness but cannot manufacture a positive population residual from a latent target already screened off by the physical descriptor. Read P72.

P73: When can target-channel stability be recovered rather than assumed?

Under one explicit three-view binary latent model, nondegenerate population moments recover the latent channel up to the unavoidable common label swap; two uncalibrated views do not generally identify the individual reliabilities. Read P73.

Scientific boundary

P100 remains a conditional sequential model-rejection theorem under its declared fresh-round, conditional e-value validity, and predictable reserve-stake assumptions. P99 remains its immediate distributed-evidence predecessor under independent-block, own-fold-exclusion, frozen-plan, and exact calibration conditions. P95 remains the drift-aware predecessor under declared finite-range dependence and common-marginal assumptions. None of these results identifies the latent state with consciousness, establishes nonphysicality, validates a replacement theory, or closes the physical-to-experiential bridge. The current mathematics narrows what a declared model can explain; it does not convert model rejection into an ontological conclusion.
