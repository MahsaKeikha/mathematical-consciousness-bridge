What remains open

The mathematical program can formalize and reject declared bridge candidates. It does not yet provide a validated physical-to-experiential law. Progress therefore requires independently justified targets, empirical protocols that can distinguish candidate theories, and careful separation between model rejection and ontological interpretation.
