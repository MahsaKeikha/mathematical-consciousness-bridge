Sixty-six results, one dependency-aware scientific program

The proposition numbers record development order. This page reorganizes the results by the scientific job they perform so that readers can see the logic of the program without reading every result sequentially.

I · Formal bridge foundations

P1-P10: What would make a bridge claim mathematically identifiable?

P1-P3

Representation, identifiability, equivalence

Establish invariance requirements, empirical identifiability, and observational equivalence classes for bridge descriptions.

P4-P6

Discrimination and sufficiency

Formalize discriminating experiment design, feature sufficiency, and a canonical bridge signature under declared assumptions.

P7-P10

Recovery under finite data

Move from ideal recovery to robust finite-error recovery, sample complexity, and robust protocol design.

II · Structured physical candidates

P11-P18: Causal structure, time, composition, and scale

P11-P13

Intervention-resolved causal structure

Replace a single scalar candidate with structured intervention response geometry and test component insufficiency and irredundancy.

P14-P16

Temporal continuation and composition

Formalize persistence through time, finite-error temporal certification, and an independent-composition null with coupling defect.

P17-P18

Coarse-graining and reconstruction

Quantify information loss under deterministic coarse-graining and establish reconstruction-based scale-sufficiency control.

III · Direct physical sufficiency tests

P19-P24: Does a declared target factor through the declared physics?

P19

Exact factorization criterion

State deterministic and stochastic conditions for whether an independently defined target factors through a declared physical descriptor.

P20-P22

Finite-sample residual certification

Convert population residuals into finite-sample certificates and extend them across nested descriptor refinement chains.

P23-P24

Adaptive selection and repeated looks

Protect validity under data-dependent descriptor selection, repeated inspection, and finite stopping rules.

IV · Operational scale consistency

P25-P37: What survives changes of physical resolution?

P25-P30

Node and state aggregation

Transport directed influence, partition irreducibility, response geometry, and the combined P11 structure through changing node/state descriptions.

P31-P34

Intervention and delay quotienting

Give compatibility criteria and ambiguity bounds when intervention labels and delays are themselves coarsened.

P35-P37

Approximate operational scale

Propagate controlled quotient ambiguity through all P11 branches and assemble a complete approximate scale certificate.

V · Quantum operational interface

P38-P44: What can and cannot be inferred from finite quantum descriptions?

P38-P39

Quantum operational sufficiency

Specialize the factorization question to tomographically complete finite-dimensional quantum descriptors, including finite-data model-set uncertainty.

P40-P42

Continuous-region regularity

Show why sampled-state injectivity alone is insufficient and derive computable regularity obstructions and finite-sample requirements.

P43-P44

Quantum/target resource allocation

Optimize uncertainty allocation and extend inference to predeclared families with data-dependent witness selection on a shared confidence event.

VI · Sequential experimental design

P45-P53: How should evidence acquisition be allocated over a graph?

P45-P46

Shared preparation graph

Optimize shared preparation streams across incident candidate edges, then characterize the hard discrete budget layer.

P47-P51

Adaptive validity and stopping

Establish anytime-valid graph refinement and convert local evidence thresholds into global stopping bounds under explicit service assumptions.

P52-P53

Capacity and residual demand

Solve minimax continuous service allocation and re-optimize exactly as residual evidence demands shrink.

VII · Execution geometry and calibration

P54-P66: From switching costs to robust measurement allocation

P54-P57

Metric execution schedules

Reduce optimal batched execution to a metric route problem and establish monotonicity, start-point stability, and metric perturbation control.

P58-P61

Noisy metric calibration

Build finite-data route envelopes, derive optimal continuous calibration, certify integer rounding, and solve the equal-cost integer problem exactly.

P62-P66

Heterogeneous-cost calibration

Separate sensitivity from measurement cost, solve the unrestricted integer problem exactly with P63, then provide scalable certified approximations and bounded-residual exact augmentation through P66.

How to audit any result

External physics or empirical dependencies belong to the bibliographies and reference audit, not to the repository-original theorem label.
