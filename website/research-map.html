Status discipline

Proved results are mathematical consequences under declared assumptions. Conditional results remain conditional on the declared physical, statistical, or measurement model. The Open bridge target is the physical-to-experiential connection itself. No proposition number converts a conditional model theorem into an empirical fact about consciousness.

P84 exact witness

For the named pair H({2,4},0) and H({2,3,4},1), the exact P75 contrast interval is [0, 1/2]. The empirical contrast is -1/4, so the gap to the complete feasible interval is 1/4. The signed contrast has eight nonzero full-law coefficients, giving the certified lower bound 1/32.

P83 remains zero on the same box. The improvement is therefore not a restatement of the previous certificate: it detects a shared-parameter incompatibility that separate parity intervals do not expose.

Scientific interpretation boundary

P71-P73 are target-side methodology results, not calibration theorems. P74-P84 continue that target-measurement/model-adequacy branch. P84's strict improvement is a statement about distance from the declared P75 statistical family under exact box constraints. It does not identify the latent variable with consciousness, and rejection of P75 would not by itself imply that consciousness is outside physics.
