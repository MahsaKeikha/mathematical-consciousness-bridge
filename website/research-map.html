How to read this research

Three kinds of status appear throughout the program.

The repository is easiest to follow when mathematical status, empirical status, and experiential interpretation are kept separate.

Proved results

Mathematical conclusions under stated assumptions

These are theorem-level claims with proofs and, where applicable, executable certificates.

Conditional results

Valid only for the declared model or sampling design

A correct theorem does not make its modeling assumptions true in nature.

Open bridge target

The physical-to-experiential step is not closed

No proposition in the repository licenses identifying a latent state, parity functional, or physical descriptor with consciousness.

Layer 1 · Physical foundations

What physical description are we actually talking about?

P1-P18 develop representation invariance, identifiability, intervention-resolved structure, temporal continuation, composition, and scale. P25-P44 extend the operational picture through scale compatibility and a quantum branch.

Why this comes first

A bridge claim cannot be stronger than the physical information it actually uses. Before asking whether a physical description explains an experiential distinction, the description itself must be clear about variables, dynamics, interventions, boundaries, time, and scale.

Layer 2 · Physical sufficiency

Does the declared physical descriptor preserve every distinction required by the target?

P19-P24 formulate exact, stochastic, differential, finite-sample, and adaptive versions of the sufficiency question.

The core test

If two cases are indistinguishable under the declared physical descriptor but remain distinguishable under an independently specified target, then that descriptor is not sufficient for that target.

Layer 6 · Held-out validation · P88

Can a discovery-selected result survive independent data?

P88 is the current theorem frontier. Discovery may choose the P75 box and one P87 functional. The pair is then frozen before an independent validation sample is examined.

Current theorem frontier: P88

Because the selected scalar score is fixed before validation, the held-out test does not require a 39,600-way functional union bound. For the stored exact gap, the P79-certified minimum validation size is 1063.

Boundary: this is box-specific unless a separate global covering argument is supplied, and genuine discovery/validation independence is essential.

Where the map stops

The physical-to-experiential bridge remains the open scientific target.

The program makes target provenance, measurement validity, model adequacy, finite-data uncertainty, and computational certification more explicit. None of those achievements, separately or together, automatically identifies a physical variable or statistical latent state with experience.
