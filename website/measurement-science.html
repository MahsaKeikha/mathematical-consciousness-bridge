Research III · Consciousness Measurement Science

A formal measurement framework for claims about consciousness

Research III treats consciousness measurement as an inference problem. An investigator observes reports, behavior, neural activity, perturbational responses, physiology, interventions, and context. The experiential target is not directly available in the same way. The scientific question is what can be identified, bounded, predicted, or falsified about that declared target under explicit assumptions.

Formal measurement problem

Separate the experiential target from the variables used to study it

For time \(t\), let \(E_t\) denote an experiential presence or state target and \(Y_t\) experiential content or phenomenal structure when defined. The investigator observes report \(R_t\), behavior \(B_t\), passive neural measurements \(N_t\), perturbational response \(P_t\), autonomic or ocular physiology \(A_t\), interventions \(I_t\), and context or confounds \(C_t\).

\[D_t=(R_t,B_t,N_t,P_t,A_t,I_t,C_t).\]

\[p(R,B,N,P,A\mid E,Y,I,C,\theta).\]

The central question is not whether one of these observables can be renamed consciousness. It is whether \(E\) or \(Y\) is identifiable from \(D\) under a defensible assumption set \(\mathcal A\).

\[\mathcal I_E(D,\mathcal A)=\left\{e:\exists\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal A)\text{ such that }p(D\mid E=e,\theta)>0\right\}.\]

ID

Point identified

The evidence and declared model support one target value within the stated model class.

PI

Partially identified

The evidence licenses an interval or set of target values, but not a unique value.

NI

Non-identified

Several target states remain observationally compatible under the allowed assumptions.

What Research III contributes

An auditable chain from evidence to claim

The program is designed so that every inferential step can be inspected separately. It does not rely on a single biomarker or a single scalar score.

01

Target-specific measurement

Presence, global state, content, phenomenal structure, and capacity are treated as different scientific targets. Validation for one target is not automatically transferred to another.

02

Consciousness Evidence Profile

The CEP records channel values, quality, uncertainty, assumptions, validation domain, and the strongest claim licensed by the evidence.

03

Identification before scoring

Point estimates are reported only when the evidence and assumptions identify them. Otherwise the output is a bound, identification region, competing explanation, or abstention.

04

Dependence-aware multimodal inference

The software distinguishes a declared conditional-independence fusion model from dependence-robust partial identification with sharp Fréchet-Hoeffding bounds.

05

Structural measurement

Phenomenal and physical relational structures are constructed independently and compared through preregistered held-out mappings rather than assumed to be identical.

06

Claim discipline

The M0-M7 ladder separates condition differences, prediction, transport, causal evidence, latent-target inference, structural prediction, and theory-discriminating tests.

Consciousness Evidence Profile

A measurement record should preserve uncertainty, assumptions, and scope

The CEP is a structured research object, not a universal consciousness score. For system \(s\), time window \(t\), declared target \(\tau\), and context \(c\),

\[\operatorname{CEP}(s,t,\tau,c)=(\mathbf v,\mathbf q,\mathbf u,\mathcal A,\mathcal D,\Gamma).\]

Here \(\mathbf v\) is the evidence vector, \(\mathbf q\) records quality and interpretability, \(\mathbf u\) records uncertainty, \(\mathcal A\) is the assumption set, \(\mathcal D\) is the validation domain, and \(\Gamma\) is the strongest allowed claim level.

The CEP can represent supporting, opposing, mixed or dissociated, and inconclusive evidence without silently converting missing or uninterpretable channels into evidence for absence.

Multimodal inference

Dependence assumptions determine what fusion is allowed to claim

For channels \(Y_1,\ldots,Y_K\), the general fused model is

\[p(E\mid Y_1,\ldots,Y_K,C)\propto p(E\mid C)\,p(Y_1,\ldots,Y_K\mid E,C).\]

Two implemented fusion regimes: when conditional independence is scientifically justified and explicitly declared, the point-fusion helper returns a likelihood ratio and posterior point estimate. When dependence is unknown, the partial-identification module instead uses sharp marginal-only Fréchet-Hoeffding bounds to return compatible intervals for the observed pattern probability, likelihood ratio, and posterior.

An interval as wide as [0,1] is a valid scientific result: it means the available marginal calibration does not identify the posterior without stronger dependence information. Infinite likelihood-ratio bounds are handled explicitly rather than clipped into a convenient finite score.

Why this matters

Report, behavior, neural signals, physiology, and perturbational measures can share dependence through arousal, medication, site, pathology, stimulus, task, or acquisition. Treating them as independent without justification can produce overconfident evidence.

Phenomenal structure

Test relational correspondence without assuming ontological identity

For communicative participants, Research III constructs a phenomenal relational space \((\mathcal E,d_E)\) from similarity, discrimination, ordering, or structured-report data and a physical or neural relational space \((\mathcal N,d_N)\) from independently defined measurements.

A preregistered mapping family \(f:\mathcal N\to\mathcal E\) is evaluated on held-out relations. One implemented normalized distortion statistic is

\[D(f)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal P|}\sum_{(i,j)\in\mathcal P}\left|\tilde d_E(e_i,e_j)-\tilde d_N(n_i,n_j)\right|.\]

Low held-out distortion supports the declared correspondence family. High distortion rejects that family. Neither outcome, by itself, establishes that physical structure and experience are ontologically identical.

Structural measurement pipeline

The test is predictive and falsifiable: the mapping is frozen before held-out phenomenal relations are evaluated.

Evidence-to-claim control

Stronger conclusions require stronger designs, not stronger wording

The M0-M7 ladder prevents a descriptive association from being presented as a causal, latent-variable, structural, or theory-discriminating result.

M0-M1

Description and held-out prediction

Establish condition differences and out-of-sample prediction before moving to stronger interpretations.

M2-M4

Transport and causal stress tests

Require generalization across participants, sites, states, confound controls, and causal perturbations.

M5-M6

Latent target and structure

License identification or bounds for a declared experiential target and held-out physical-to-phenomenal structural prediction only under explicit assumptions.

M7

Theory discrimination

Require a preregistered prediction that is genuinely divergent across candidate theories and survives while relevant rivals fail.

NEG

Negative evidence

A failed channel counts against a target only when negative sensitivity, acquisition quality, failure behavior, and domain match are independently characterized.

ABSTAIN

Inconclusive is an allowed result

When identification, quality, validation, or assumptions are insufficient, the scientifically correct output is to abstain rather than manufacture certainty.

Scope of current evidence

What is established here, and what is not

Established in the current Research III record

The repository specifies a formal measurement model, explicit identification regions, target separation, the CEP data contract, an M0-M7 claim registry, an Assumption registry, a Failure-mode registry, dependence-aware fusion scaffolds, structural-alignment tests, a prospective validation program, machine-readable schemas, and reproducibility checks.

These are methodological and computational results. They define how stronger measurement claims can be stated, audited, challenged, and eventually validated.

Claims not licensed by the current record

The repository does not establish a universal consciousness biomarker, a direct third-person readout of qualia, a validated clinical diagnostic device, or an ontological identity between a physical variable and experience.

It also does not establish that one biological, computational, dynamical, informational, or quantum condition is sufficient for consciousness across systems.

Research III

The objective is not to produce a more impressive consciousness score. It is to make every measurement claim scientifically inspectable.

Research III therefore treats assumptions, uncertainty, non-identification, validation domain, causal evidence, and falsification as part of the result rather than as caveats added after the fact.
