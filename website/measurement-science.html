Research III · Consciousness Measurement Science

What would it take to measure consciousness without defining it by the signal we happen to record?

Research III is the measurement-science stage of the program. It asks what reports, behavior, neural measurements, perturbational responses, physiology, interventions, and context can scientifically justify about explicitly declared experiential targets while keeping uncertainty, dependence, confounding, and non-identifiability visible.

Central measurement question

Experience is the target. Evidence is what third-person science can observe.

Let \(E\) denote an experiential target. Let \(R\) be report, \(B\) behavior, \(N\) neural measurements, \(P\) perturbational responses, \(A\) autonomic and ocular physiology, \(I\) interventions, and \(C\) context.

\[D=(R,B,N,P,A,I,C).\]

\[\boxed{\text{Given }D\text{ and assumptions }\mathcal A,\;\text{what can be identified, bounded, predicted, or falsified about }E?}\]

Identification rule: if the observations and assumptions do not determine a unique experiential conclusion, the scientifically permitted output is a bound, an identification region, competing explanations, or an inconclusive result. The program does not manufacture a point estimate merely because one is convenient.

Implemented now versus not yet established

Separate the research infrastructure from the empirical result we still need to earn

Implemented now

Target taxonomy: presence, global state, content, phenomenal structure, and capacity are kept distinct.

Evidence object: the Consciousness Evidence Profile records values, interpretability, uncertainty, validation domain, assumptions, allowed claims, and forbidden claims.

Claim control: the M0-M7 ladder separates descriptive signal differences from calibrated prediction, transport, causal testing, latent-target inference, structural prediction, and theory discrimination.

Machine-readable governance: CEP and claim schemas, examples, an assumption registry, failure-mode registry, reproducibility rules, and repository policy are source controlled.

Software scaffolds: conditional-independence likelihood-ratio fusion, dependence-robust partial identification with sharp Fréchet-Hoeffding bounds, evidence-profile objects, and structural-alignment tools are implemented and tested.

Not yet established

No universal consciousness biomarker has been validated.

No single scalar has been shown to preserve meaning across targets, states, populations, species, or substrates.

No empirical program in this repository has yet demonstrated that a specific biological, computational, dynamical, informational, or quantum condition is sufficient for experience.

No phenomenal-to-physical mapping has yet earned ontological identity status.

No current result settles whether consciousness is emergent, fundamental, dual-aspect, fully physical, or otherwise.

Five measurement targets

Do not collapse distinct scientific questions into one number

1

Presence

What evidence supports that any experience is occurring under the declared scope?

2

Global state

What multidimensional condition characterizes arousal, connectedness, complexity, stability, and cognitive availability?

3

Content

What is being experienced, and how well can that content be predicted or decoded under validated conditions?

4

Phenomenal structure

What relational organization holds among reported qualities, similarities, discriminations, and experiential dimensions?

5

Capacity

Does the system have causal or dynamical organization associated with supporting experience even when content cannot be accessed?

Research arm A

State, content, and capacity inference

The first arm builds a target-specific evidence profile instead of declaring one signal to be consciousness. Each channel carries its own value, interpretability, uncertainty, validation domain, quality notes, and rules governing whether a negative observation can count as evidence against the target.

\[\mathbf V=(V_R,V_B,V_N,V_P,V_A).\]

When fusion is justified, the general form is

\[p(E\mid Y_1,\ldots,Y_K,C)\propto p(E\mid C)\,p(Y_1,\ldots,Y_K\mid E,C).\]

Conditional independence is not the default scientific assumption. Unknown dependence must be modeled, bounded, or stress-tested.

Negative evidence rule

An uninterpretable or negative channel does not by itself support absence of experience. Negative evidence is only scientifically meaningful when sensitivity and failure behavior are separately validated for the target, state, population, and measurement context.

Research arm B

Phenomenal structure measurement

For communicative participants, the program constructs a phenomenal relational space from similarity judgments, discrimination, ordering, and structured reports, then compares it with a physical or neural relational space built from population activity, effective connectivity, perturbational responses, or representational geometry.

\[D(f)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal P|}\sum_{(i,j)\in\mathcal P}\left|\tilde d_E(e_i,e_j)-\tilde d_N(n_i,n_j)\right|.\]

Low held-out distortion supports the declared correspondence family. High distortion counts against that mapping family. Neither result alone proves that neural structure is experience.

Candidate mathematical tools

Representational similarity, multidimensional scaling, optimal transport, Gromov-Wasserstein comparison, persistent homology, category theory, and local-to-global sheaf constructions are admissible only when their empirical role and risky predictions are explicit.

Experimental program

Move from benchmark calibration to cross-state, causal, clinical, structural, and adversarial tests

Sleep and altered states

Statistical validation

A candidate measure must earn transport, calibration, incremental value, and a documented failure domain

Primary analysis should keep discovery, internal confirmation, and external confirmation separate. Repeated measures remain grouped by participant. A model must report proper scoring loss, calibration, uncertainty, abstention, and transport rather than relying on accuracy alone.

One explicit cross-state endpoint is

\[\Delta_{\mathrm{transport}}=\mathcal L_{\mathrm{new\ state}}-\mathcal L_{\mathrm{reference}}.\]

Multimodal fusion must demonstrate prospective incremental value over the strongest locked single-channel baseline. Unknown channel dependence should widen conclusions, not disappear from the analysis.

Abstention is a scientific output

Quality-control failure, out-of-domain inputs, unstable identification, and unresolved model disagreement should produce an uninterpretable or inconclusive result rather than an invented negative label.

Software and reproducibility

The code is deliberately modest and auditable

Software guide

What the current evidence, profile, partial-identification, and structural-alignment modules do and do not implement.

Reproducibility

Environment, tests, schemas, examples, deterministic checks, and publication discipline.

Repository policy

Automated constraints on links, placeholders, machine-readable artifacts, reader surfaces, and text hygiene.

Test suite

Unit tests for point fusion, dependence-robust partial identification, profile semantics, structural alignment, schemas, and repository policy.

Two implemented fusion regimes: when conditional independence is scientifically justified and explicitly declared, the point-fusion helper returns a likelihood ratio and posterior point estimate. When dependence is unknown, the partial-identification module instead uses sharp marginal-only Fréchet-Hoeffding bounds to return compatible intervals for the observed pattern probability, likelihood ratio, and posterior.

An interval as wide as [0,1] is a valid scientific result: it means the available marginal calibration does not identify the posterior without stronger dependence information. Infinite likelihood-ratio bounds are handled explicitly rather than clipped into a convenient finite score.

Scientific boundary

Research III is a measurement program, not an ontological shortcut

No ontology by assumption

Physicalist, emergentist, dual-aspect, fundamental, and other interpretations remain open unless evidence distinguishes them.

No universal biomarker by declaration

EEG complexity, PCI, connectivity, report, behavior, or any other marker must be validated for a declared target and domain.

No response is not no experience

Motor failure, task failure, memory failure, or an insensitive neural test can erase evidence without erasing experience.

No quantum shortcut

A quantum-specific account must make discriminating predictions that outperform relevant classical alternatives.

No hidden dependence assumption

Multimodal fusion must model or stress-test channel dependence rather than silently multiplying evidence as if channels were independent.

No clinical overreach

The repository is not a medical device and does not replace specialist evaluation or justify life-support decisions.

The complete progression

Research I defines a physical observer candidate. Research II disciplines the bridge. Research III builds the measurement science.

The three stages are intentionally distinct. A recovered physical subsystem is not automatically a conscious subject. A bridge theorem is not automatically a consciousness meter. A calibrated biomarker is not automatically an ontological identity. Keeping those handoffs explicit is what makes the program falsifiable.
