Mathematical physics · statistical inference · experimental design

A testable framework for the physical-to-experiential bridge problem

This project asks what mathematical and physical conditions would be required before a complete physical description could support a scientifically testable claim about consciousness. It does not begin by assuming what consciousness is. The current target-side frontier formalizes non-circular target provenance, noisy target measurement, and one explicit population identifiability theorem for recovering target-channel stability from three independent views.

Scientific boundary

The repository does not claim that consciousness has already been derived from physics, that consciousness is necessarily quantum, that consciousness is a new dimension of spacetime, or that any familiar scalar quantity is identical to experience. Those hypotheses require independent mathematical derivation and empirical support.

The current standard is narrower: the physical descriptor, target-construction protocol, target-measurement model, identifiability assumptions, bridge class, and uncertainty model must each be declared rather than hidden inside one score.

Current theorem frontier

P71-P73 strengthen the target side of the bridge test

P71 asks whether the target is non-circular. P72 asks whether noisy observation preserves its distinctions. P73 asks whether the channel stability used by P72 can itself be identified rather than assumed.

P71

Target-provenance non-circularity

If a target is constructed as a function or descriptor-only channel of the tested physical descriptor, factorization or screening-off holds by construction.

P72

Target-measurement robustness

Under a nondifferential target channel, \(I(Y;\Omega\mid T)\le I(E^\star;\Omega\mid T)\). Measurement can attenuate or erase a real witness but cannot create the population residual from nothing under the stated model.

P73

Three-view channel identifiability

For a nondegenerate binary latent target with three conditionally independent binary views, observable second and third moments recover the view channels up to a global latent-label swap. The P72 stability coefficients \(\gamma_j=|b_j|\) are therefore identifiable.

P19-P24

Core physical sufficiency

Exact factorization, conditional-information residuals, finite-sample certification, descriptor refinement, adaptive selection, and repeated-look validity define the core test lineage.

P38-P44

Quantum sufficiency branch

Operational quantum completeness is separated from experiential completeness, with finite tomography and regularity-aware non-factorization tests.

P61-P70

Calibration branch

Downstream resource-allocation mathematics remains separately documented and does not define the target or bridge.

Falsification and evidence

The framework is designed to fail informatively

A scientific bridge proposal should expose conditions under which its descriptor, target, measurement model, identifiability assumptions, or bridge class is shown inadequate.

Target circularity

Reject evidential use when the target construction already forces factorization through the tested descriptor.

Measurement contamination

Reject P72 transfer if the observed target depends on the underlying state beyond the declared latent target and descriptor.

Identifiability failure

Do not claim recovered channel reliability when the P73 multi-view assumptions are degenerate or unsupported. Two uncalibrated views are insufficient in general.

Witness erasure

Treat a null result as inconclusive when the target channel has insufficient stability to preserve latent distinctions.

Descriptor collision

Find same-descriptor cases whose independently justified target distributions remain distinguishable.

Finite-data failure

Do not promote an empirical gap when its confidence lower bound remains zero.
