Mathematical physics · statistical inference · experimental design

A testable framework for the physical-to-experiential bridge problem

This project asks what mathematical and physical conditions would be required before a complete physical description could support a scientifically testable claim about consciousness. It does not begin by assuming what consciousness is. It builds the machinery needed to test physical sufficiency, distinguishability, reconstructability, scale consistency, finite-data reliability, and falsifiability.

Scientific boundary

The repository does not claim that consciousness has already been derived from physics, that consciousness is necessarily quantum, that consciousness is a new dimension of spacetime, or that any familiar scalar quantity is identical to experience. Those are hypotheses that would require independent mathematical derivation and empirical support.

The central standard is narrower and more rigorous: given a declared physical descriptor and an independently defined target, can the target be shown to factor through that physical descriptor, or can an experiment demonstrate that the descriptor is insufficient?

Foundations

Physics first, interpretation second

The framework separates the physical system from the experiential interpretation. A physical model specifies a state space, admissible dynamics or channels, interventions, measurements, and probability laws. Only after that structure is explicit does the project ask whether an independently defined target is determined by it.

\[\text{physical dynamics}\to\text{operational structure}\to\text{controlled interventions}\to\text{response geometry}\to\text{finite-data test}\to\text{bridge question}.\]

Current theorem frontier

From uncertainty to executable experimental design

P58-P69 form the newest quantitative chain. They move from noisy transition measurements to optimal calibration allocations under increasingly realistic resource constraints.

P58

Noisy transition-metric envelopes

Finite measurements generate simultaneous lower and upper route envelopes, supporting a robust route and a worst-case regret certificate.

P59

Continuous calibration optimum

Under an inverse-square-root uncertainty surrogate, the optimal effort scales with the two-thirds power of the edge uncertainty-sensitivity coefficient.

P60

Certified integer rounding

A practical whole-measurement allocation is obtained with an explicit multiplicative overhead bound.

P61

Exact equal-cost integer allocation

Diminishing marginal gains make a priority-queue greedy allocation globally optimal under equal per-measurement costs.

P62

Heterogeneous measurement costs

When cost varies by edge, the exact continuous allocation separates scientific sensitivity from experimental expense.

P63

Exact heterogeneous integer design

A Bellman dynamic program solves the whole-measurement problem exactly for positive integer costs, with gcd budget compression.

P64

Fast certified approximation

Flooring the continuous optimum gives a scalable feasible design with an explicit approximation factor in its declared validity regime.

P65

Baseline-safe water filling

Mandatory one-sample constraints enter the continuous optimization directly, yielding an exact active-set optimum and a universally square-root-of-two certified floor approximation.

P66

Residual-exact augmentation

The budget left after P65 flooring is bounded by the baseline cost, enabling exact residual optimization inside the floor-dominating integer class without a full-budget dynamic-program axis.

P67

Global optimality certificate

A common discrete marginal multiplier can certify that a budget-tight integer candidate is the unrestricted P63 optimum. Failure of the sufficient test remains inconclusive.

P68

Lagrangian optimality gap

Any positive multiplier gives a rigorous lower bound on the unrestricted P63 optimum and therefore a quantitative candidate-gap certificate. P67 is the zero-gap special case.

P69

Certified dual optimization

Concavity, exact supergradient intervals, and supporting-line bounds optimize the P68 multiplier family to a certified dual-value tolerance without assuming strong duality.

Falsification and evidence

The framework is designed to fail informatively

A scientific bridge proposal should not merely fit known observations. It should expose conditions under which a declared physical descriptor would be shown insufficient for a declared target.

Descriptor collision

Find physically equivalent cases under the declared descriptor whose target distributions remain distinguishable.

Scale failure

Show that a coarse physical description discards target-relevant structure that cannot be reconstructed within the declared tolerance.

Regularity obstruction

Demonstrate target separation exceeding what a declared continuous bridge class can support inside the physical confidence region.

Finite-data certification

Quantify the confidence required before treating an apparent violation as evidence rather than sampling noise.

The standard is a complete chain of justified inference

The project should be judged by explicit assumptions, valid mathematics, accurate physics, transparent empirical interfaces, reproducible computation, discriminating experiments, and citation discipline. When a conclusion is open, it is labeled open. When a theorem is conditional, its conditions remain visible.
