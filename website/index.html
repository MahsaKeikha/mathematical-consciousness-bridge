Mathematical physics · statistical inference · measurement science · falsifiable experiment design

What would a scientifically testable bridge from physical description to experience actually require?

This project treats that question as a three-stage scientific program. First identify the physical system being studied. Then test whether a declared physical description is sufficient for an independently defined target. Then ask what multimodal evidence can actually identify, bound, predict, or falsify about experience while uncertainty and non-identification remain visible.

The whole research program in three stages

Find the physical system. Test the proposed bridge. Make the evidence measurable.

The project is deliberately layered so that no stage quietly assumes the answer to the next one. Research I asks what physical subsystem is being tracked. Research II asks whether a declared physical description preserves every distinction required by an independently specified target. Research III asks what observable evidence can support about experiential targets under explicit uncertainty and dependence assumptions.

Research I · Physical subsystem identification

What physical system are we actually following through time?

Research I develops a moving-boundary observer mathematics. Its object is not “consciousness”; it is the physical subsystem itself. The program asks whether a candidate world-tube can be inferred from stochastic dynamics using integration, insulation, persistence, transport, global path optimization, identifiability, and finite-sample certification.

Research I pipeline

Measured observables → stochastic dynamics → candidate boundary → world-tube recovery

The operational question is whether one moving subsystem remains meaningfully identifiable while the measured coordinates carrying it change.

Research II · Current theorem frontier · P92

Exact global mixed-prevalence distance

P92 closes the P91 full-cube bracket. Three conditional two-by-two determinants on the `X1 = 1` subtensor have a nonnegative product for every two-component P75 mixture.

Universal nonlinear invariant

For every P75 law, the selected determinant product is nonnegative.

Exact empirical obstruction

The empirical determinant signs are (-, +, +). Their exact stability radii are 1/24, 3/56, 5/72.

Exact global closure

No P75 law lies closer than 1/24, and the mixed P91 point attains it. Therefore d_inf = 1/24.

Scientific boundary: P92 is an exact conditional model-separation theorem. It does not identify consciousness, establish nonphysicality, validate an alternative theory, or close the physical-to-experiential bridge.

Research III · Consciousness measurement science

What can third-person evidence actually justify about experience?

Research III moves from bridge logic to measurement science. It separates the experiential target from the evidence used to infer it and asks what reports, behavior, neural measurements, perturbational responses, physiology, interventions, and context can identify, bound, predict, or falsify under explicit assumptions.

Measurement architecture

Experience is the target. Evidence is what science can observe.

Research III keeps five measurement targets separate and requires uncertainty, interpretability, validation domain, assumptions, allowed claims, and forbidden claims to travel with the evidence.

Open the full Research III website guide →

5 separated targets

Presence, global state, content, phenomenal structure, and capacity are treated as distinct scientific questions rather than collapsed into one score.

Two implemented fusion regimes

Point fusion is available when conditional independence is justified; dependence-robust partial identification uses sharp Fréchet-Hoeffding bounds when it is not.

Non-identification is a result

An interval as wide as [0,1] can be scientifically correct when the observations and assumptions do not identify one answer.

Scientific boundary: Research III is a theory-neutral measurement architecture and tested software foundation. It is not a validated universal consciousness meter, and it does not settle the ontology of consciousness.

The project in plain language

The goal is not to declare a consciousness formula. It is to make bridge claims scientifically testable.

The program asks three different questions that are often mixed together: what physical system is being studied, whether a chosen physical description is sufficient for an independent target, and what evidence can justify about experiential claims. Keeping those questions separate makes it possible to expose hidden assumptions, detect non-identification, design discriminating experiments, and say “open” when the evidence does not decide.

Shared scientific boundary

What the combined program has not established

The repository does not claim that consciousness has already been derived from physics. Research I does not turn a recovered physical subsystem into a conscious subject. Research II does not turn conditional model separation into proof of consciousness or nonphysicality. Research III does not turn a measurement scaffold into a universal consciousness meter.

The physical-to-experiential bridge remains an independently testable open problem. The purpose of the three-stage architecture is to narrow that problem without hiding the missing step inside a definition.
