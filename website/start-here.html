First-reader guide

From the simple question to the actual mathematics

This page is the bridge between the public explanation and the formal research. The project asks what must be true before a physical description can support a scientifically testable claim about an independently specified experiential target. It does not assume a formula for consciousness. It builds mathematical tests for the assumptions that such a claim would have to survive.

The most important boundary

This repository does not claim that consciousness has been derived from physics. A theorem about a declared model is not automatically a theorem about consciousness, a latent variable is not automatically experience, a failed descriptor is not proof of nonphysical consciousness, and failure to reject a model is not model validation.

Before the equations

Six terms that make the rest of the project easier to follow

You do not need the proposition chronology to understand the logic. These six ideas recur throughout the research and are used consistently.

Physical descriptor

The declared physical information used by a bridge claim: variables, dynamics, interventions, scale, and system boundary.

Target

An independently specified distinction the physical description is being asked to explain. The target must not be manufactured from the tested descriptor and then treated as independent evidence.

Latent state

An unobserved variable introduced by a statistical model. A latent state has no experiential meaning merely because it is mathematically useful.

Observation channel

The measurement process connecting a latent target to reports, labels, behavior, or other observed variables. It can attenuate or erase information.

Compatibility and rejection

Compatibility means the declared model has not been ruled out by the current certificate. Rejection means the declared model is incompatible under stated assumptions. Neither statement identifies consciousness.

Bridge claim

The still-open scientific step connecting a sufficiently complete physical description to experiential structure. The repository studies obligations on such a claim without assuming that the bridge is already known.

The formal question

What does “physically sufficient” mean here?

At the deterministic level, the project asks whether an independently specified target \(E\) can be determined entirely from a declared physical descriptor \(T\):

\[E=B\circ T.\]

At the stochastic level, the analogous question is whether any target information remains once the physical descriptor is known:

\[I(E;\Omega\mid T)=0.\]

These are not definitions of consciousness. They are mathematical sufficiency conditions for a declared descriptor and a declared target under explicit assumptions.

Plain-language interpretation

If two cases have the same declared physical description but the independently defined target still distinguishes them, then that physical description is not sufficient for that target.

The scientifically correct next question is then what the description omitted or which assumption failed.

Research II · Current certified frontier

P92-P99 connect exact population separation to dependence-aware, selection-valid certification

P92 closes the mixed-prevalence population distance exactly. Three selected determinant signs imply the exact full-cube distance d_inf(P_emp, M75) = 1/24.

P93 carries that obstruction into finite IID data. A simultaneous seven-cell confidence event yields the 95 percent mathematical crossing at 1623 samples, with the first exact 24-count replication clearing at 1632.

P94 allows a declared finite dependence range. The same sign-coherence witness remains certifiable under an m-dependent stream with one common marginal law, while an exact counterexample shows why arbitrary temporal drift cannot simply be pooled away.

P95 handles declared drift by stratification. Predeclared regimes receive local finite-range certificates and one familywise error budget, allowing their marginal laws to differ without pretending the pooled stream is stationary.

P96 separates adaptive plan selection from final certification. Pilot information may choose the regime plan, but that plan must be frozen before a genuinely independent holdout sample is inspected.

P97 permits same-data selection inside one predeclared finite candidate family. It pays explicitly for the search through simultaneous candidate-level error accounting.

P98 is the immediate predecessor. It rotates the P96 holdout principle across mutually independent data blocks. In fold k, the plan may use the other blocks, but block k is excluded from its own plan-selection rule and is inspected only after that plan is frozen. The final fold certificates may be dependent because their selection information overlaps; one outer union bound controls them simultaneously.

Exact P98 checkpoint: for two folds, two one-step-dependent regimes per fold, and 95 percent global confidence, the crossing is 4045 observations per regime, the first exact denominator-24 replication is 4056, and the two-fold unique-data totals are 16180 / 16224.

P99 is the current frontier. It converts each selection-valid cross-fitted fold rejection into an exact e-value and combines fold evidence by a fixed convex average. The validity argument uses conditional fold validity, expectation linearity, and Markov's inequality, so the final fold certificates do not need to be independent.

Exact P99 checkpoint: for the declared two-fold, two-regime, one-step-dependent distributed-evidence design at 95 percent global confidence, the crossing is 3774 observations per regime, the first exact denominator-24 replication is 3792, and the unique-data totals are 15096 / 15168. P99 does not uniformly dominate P98.

The logical direction remains one-sided. These results can reject the declared P75 model family under their assumptions. They do not validate the model after non-rejection, identify consciousness, establish nonphysicality, or close the physical-to-experiential bridge.

Scientific interpretation

Five things the repository deliberately refuses to overclaim

01

Descriptor failure is local

If one physical descriptor is insufficient, relevant physical information may have been omitted. This is not proof of nonphysical consciousness.

02

Good fit is not a bridge

A fit may be circular, underidentified, noisy, underconstrained, or compatible with many alternative models. Additional scientific obligations remain.

03

Latent is not experiential

A latent statistical state does not inherit experiential meaning without an independent semantic and empirical anchor.

04

Quantum is not enough by itself

A complete quantum description remains a physical description. A bridge principle still has to be stated and tested.

05

Non-rejection is inconclusive

Failure to certify incompatibility is not model acceptance and does not establish a physical-to-experiential bridge.

Research II · Current frontier · P99

Several valid folds can now contribute evidence to one exact global test

P99 converts a valid level-tau cross-fitted fold rejection into the exact e-value R(tau)/tau. A finite calibration mixture remains valid when it is frozen before the fold sees its own certification evidence, and fixed convex averaging across folds remains valid without assuming the final fold certificates are independent.

In the balanced two-fold, two-regime, one-step-dependent example, fold test level 1/25 gives local regime level 1/50. The mathematical crossing is 3774 observations per regime, the first exact denominator-24 replication is 3792, and the unique two-fold totals are 15096 / 15168.

P99 does not uniformly dominate P98. Its advantage is distributed evidence. P98 can remain better when one fold is individually decisive. Own-fold leakage, post-hoc calibration search, dependent-stream pseudo-folds, model acceptance, consciousness identification, nonphysicality, and the physical-to-experiential bridge remain open.

Research II · Previous frontier · P98

Cross-fitting can reuse every independent block without letting a fold select on its own certification evidence

P98 rotates the P96 holdout principle. For each fold, the plan may be learned from the other independent blocks, but the fold's own certification block is excluded from its plan-selection rule and is inspected only after the plan is frozen. The fold certificates may be dependent because their selection information overlaps; one outer union bound keeps them simultaneous.

Exact balanced checkpoint: two folds, two regimes per fold, and one-step dependence give 4045 observations per regime at 95 percent global confidence, first exact replication at 4056, and unique-data totals 16180 / 16224.

Boundary: a random split of one dependent stream is not automatically an independent cross-fitted design. Own-fold leakage, unbudgeted scheme search, model acceptance, consciousness identification, nonphysicality, and the physical-to-experiential bridge remain open.

Research II · Historical frontier · P97

Same-data candidate selection can be valid when the entire finite search family is covered at once

P97 complements the P96 independent-holdout strategy. Before looking at certification statistics, declare a finite family of candidate regime plans. Each candidate receives its own P95 familywise error budget, and a second union bound covers all candidates simultaneously. After that simultaneous certificate is established, the final candidate can be chosen from the predeclared family using the same data without invalidating its certificate.

Exact balanced checkpoint: two candidates, two regimes per candidate, and one-step dependence give 4045 observations per regime at 95 percent global confidence; the first exact denominator-24 replication is 4056.

Boundary: a newly invented candidate after inspection is not covered. Unrestricted search, within-regime drift, model acceptance after non-rejection, consciousness identification, nonphysicality, and the physical-to-experiential bridge remain open.

Research II · Previous frontier · P96

Adaptive pilot selection is separated from final certification

P95 requires the regime plan to be fixed independently of the certification witness. P96 permits pilot information to choose that plan, freezes it before holdout evaluation, and then applies P95 to genuinely independent certification information. Conditional validity integrates to the same unconditional familywise bound, so pilot-search complexity itself adds no alpha penalty under the declared independence assumptions.

Balanced holdout checkpoint: B=2, m=1 gives 3645 observations per regime for the 95 percent mathematical crossing and 3648 for the first exact denominator-24 replication.

Boundary: a naive split of one dependent stream is not automatically independent. Same-data redesign, within-regime drift, model acceptance after non-rejection, consciousness identification, and bridge completion remain outside the theorem.

Research II · Historical drift-aware frontier · P95

Drift is handled by testing predeclared regimes instead of pooling them

P94 showed that pooling changing marginal laws can create a false qualitative sign-coherence rejection. P95 keeps the P94 finite-range certificate inside each predeclared regime, permits different marginals across regimes, and controls the full set of regime tests with one explicit familywise error budget. With two equally budgeted one-dependent regimes at 95 percent familywise confidence, the established witness crosses at 3645 observations per regime and first clears on an exact replication at 3648.

Boundary: data-dependent segmentation and unrestricted gradual drift require additional theory. Non-rejection does not establish P75, and the physical-to-experiential bridge remains open.
