# Quantitative Physics & Mathematics Atlas

> **40 equation-driven figures.** This atlas is deliberately different from the conceptual architecture diagrams elsewhere in the repository. Every figure below is generated from an explicit equation, deterministic numerical calculation, closed-form relation, or fixed-seed simulation.

## Scientific-status rule

Closed-form mathematical and physical facts are identified as such. Synthetic intervention, perturbation, network, and complexity examples are explicitly labeled **synthetic** and are not presented as empirical evidence for consciousness. The purpose of this atlas is mathematical auditing, physical intuition, reproducibility, and test design.

## Reproducibility

All figures are generated by [`scripts/generate_quantitative_atlas.py`](../scripts/generate_quantitative_atlas.py). The generated machine-readable inventory is [`quantitative_figure_manifest.json`](figures/quantitative/quantitative_figure_manifest.json). Numerical checkpoints are recorded in the [Quantitative Atlas Validation Report](quantitative_atlas_validation_report.md).

# I. Physical dynamics, stochastic processes, and thermodynamics

## Q01. Ornstein-Uhlenbeck mean-reverting trajectories

![Q01](figures/quantitative/q01_ornstein_uhlenbeck_trajectories.svg)

**Equation:** `dX = theta(mu-X)dt + sigma dW`  
**Fact / interpretation:** linear drift restores the state toward equilibrium while diffusion sustains stationary fluctuations.  
**Status:** equation-driven stochastic simulation with fixed random seed.

## Q02. OU stationary variance

![Q02](figures/quantitative/q02_ou_stationary_variance.svg)

**Equation:** `Var[X(t)] = sigma^2/(2 theta) [1-exp(-2 theta t)]`  
**Fact:** for a zero-initialized OU process, the variance approaches `sigma^2/(2 theta)` monotonically.  
**Status:** closed-form mathematical fact.

**Status:** equation-driven physical illustration.

## Q05. Linear state-space flow

# II. Probability, information theory, and information geometry

## Q14. Correlated Gaussian geometry

![Q14](figures/quantitative/q14_correlated_gaussian_contours.svg)

**Equation:** `p(x) proportional to exp[-1/2 (x-mu)^T Sigma^-1 (x-mu)]`  
**Fact:** off-diagonal covariance rotates elliptical equal-density contours, encoding statistical dependence.  
**Status:** equation-driven probability geometry.

## Q15. Gaussian-shift KL divergence

![Q15](figures/quantitative/q15_gaussian_shift_kl.svg)

**Equation:** `D_KL = (mu1-mu2)^2/(2 sigma^2)`  
**Fact:** for equal variances, Gaussian KL divergence grows quadratically with normalized mean separation.  
**Status:** closed-form information-theoretic fact.

## Q16. Bernoulli Fisher information

![Q16](figures/quantitative/q16_bernoulli_fisher_information.svg)

**Equation:** `I(p)=1/[p(1-p)]`  
**Fact:** under direct Bernoulli parameterization, Fisher information is minimal at `p=1/2` and diverges toward the boundaries.  
**Status:** closed-form information-geometry fact.

## Q17. Gaussian mutual information

![Q17](figures/quantitative/q17_gaussian_mutual_information.svg)

**Equation:** `I(X;Y)=-1/2 log(1-rho^2)`  
**Fact:** for jointly Gaussian scalar variables, mutual information depends only on `|rho|` and increases as correlation approaches unit magnitude.  
**Status:** closed-form information-theoretic fact.

## Q18. Gaussian total variation under mean shift

![Q18](figures/quantitative/q18_gaussian_total_variation.svg)

**Definition:** `TV(P,Q)=1/2 integral |p-q| dx`  
**Fact:** total variation increases from zero toward one as equal-variance Gaussian laws become more separated.  
**Status:** numerical integration of a mathematical definition.

**Status:** numerical verification of Proposition 17.

## Q26. Exact many-to-one collision

![Q26](figures/quantitative/q26_exact_coarse_graining_collision.svg)

**Witness:** `C(x)=C(x')`, `x != x'`, so `C#delta_x = C#delta_x'`.  
**Fact:** a many-to-one map can collapse maximally distinct point-mass laws into the same coarse law.  
**Status:** exact Proposition 17 counterexample.

## Q27. Reconstruction defect under decoder mismatch

![Q27](figures/quantitative/q27_reconstruction_defect_decoder_mismatch.svg)

**Equation:** `rho(P)=TV(P, R#C#P)`  
**Fact:** with fixed coarse mass, reconstruction error is governed by mismatch between the decoder and the true within-fiber conditional structure.  
**Status:** closed-form Proposition 18 example.

## Q28. P18 scale-identifiability margin
