# The Consciousness Bridge Problem

## 1. Problem statement

A physical theory supplies states, histories, observables, dynamics, and interventions. A mathematical consciousness theory additionally needs a rule connecting some of those physical structures to experiential claims.

Let

\[
\mathcal P
\]

be a declared class of physically realizable systems or histories and let

\[
\mathcal E
\]

be a formal class of experiential structures.

The consciousness bridge problem is to characterize relations

\[
\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal P\times\mathcal E
\]

that are simultaneously:

- mathematically coherent;
- representation invariant;
- physically grounded;
- empirically exposed;
- nontrivial;
- compatible with temporal and compositional structure;
- distinguishable from serious alternatives whenever distinguishability is claimed.

## 2. Why the bridge cannot be hidden

If one defines a scalar \(C(p)\) and then declares

\[
C(p)>c_0
\iff
p\text{ is conscious},
\]

the mathematical implication is true only because the right-hand concept was built into the definition.

The scientific problem is therefore not to manufacture a scalar whose threshold is called consciousness. It is to justify the bridge between physical structure and experiential interpretation.

## 3. Formal target

The project seeks a hierarchy of results:

\[
\text{coherence}
\Rightarrow
\text{representation invariance}
\Rightarrow
\text{identifiability}
\Rightarrow
\text{empirical discriminants}
\Rightarrow
\text{conditional bridge theorem}.
\]

## 4. Strongest possible outcome

A mathematically and scientifically strong result would have the form

\[
\boxed{
A_1,\ldots,A_k
+
P_1(p),\ldots,P_m(p)
\Longrightarrow
E(p)
}
\]

where:

- \(A_i\) are explicitly stated bridge principles;
- \(P_j\) are independently measurable or physically defined conditions;
- \(E(p)\) is a precisely formalized experiential claim;
- the \(A_i\) generate empirical consequences that distinguish them from serious alternatives.

The theorem would prove the implication. Empirical work would be needed to support the bridge principles and physical premises.
