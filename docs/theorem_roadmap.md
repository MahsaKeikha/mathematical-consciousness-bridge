# Theorem Roadmap

## T1. Quotient Bridge Theorem

Find necessary and sufficient conditions for a bridge relation to descend from physical descriptions to equivalence classes:

\[
p\sim_P p'
\Longrightarrow
\mathcal B(p)\sim_E\mathcal B(p').
\]

## T2. Bridge Nontriviality Lemma

Characterize when a bridge has at least two experientially distinct equivalence classes.

## T3. Bridge Identifiability Theorem

Given an experiment class \(\Pi\) and observation map

\[
\mathcal M^\Pi,
\]

characterize when two bridge theories are statistically distinguishable.

## T4. Observational Equivalence No-Go Theorem

If

\[
P_{\mathcal B_1}^{\Pi}
=
P_{\mathcal B_2}^{\Pi}
\]

for all admissible \(\Pi\), prove that the bridge choice is not identifiable within that empirical regime.

## T5. Minimal Discriminating Experiment

Define

\[
\Pi^\star
=
\arg\max_{\Pi\in\mathfrak P}
D\!\left(
P_{\mathcal B_1}^{\Pi},
P_{\mathcal B_2}^{\Pi}
\right)
\]

under physical and ethical constraints.

## T6. Composition Theorem

Given two physical subsystems \(p_1,p_2\), determine what additional axioms are needed to infer any relation between

\[
\mathcal B(p_1),\quad
\mathcal B(p_2),\quad
\mathcal B(p_1\oplus p_2).
\]

## T7. Temporal Continuation Theorem

Determine conditions under which local bridge assignments over time induce a consistent experiential history.

## T8. Observer-to-Bridge Interface Theorem

Connect a certified world-tube from Spatiotemporal Observer Mathematics to a physical equivalence class suitable as input to the bridge.

## T9. Conditional Consciousness Theorem

Only after T1-T8:

\[
\boxed{
\text{validated bridge conditions}
+
\text{certified physical conditions}
\Longrightarrow
\text{formal experiential property}.
}
\]

The word "validated" here includes both theorem-level coherence and empirical support for the bridge assumptions used.
