# Physics and Mathematics Atlas for Consciousness Research

![Physics and mathematics atlas](figures/physics_mathematics_atlas.svg)

This atlas organizes the mathematical and physical disciplines most relevant to a rigorous consciousness research program. It is designed as a **reference map**, not as a claim that one equation, one physical quantity, or one existing theory already solves the physical-to-experiential bridge.

The organizing principle is

\[
\boxed{
\text{physical law}
\longrightarrow
\text{measurable structure}
\longrightarrow
\text{mathematical invariant}
\longrightarrow
\text{empirical discrimination}
\longrightarrow
\text{bridge test}.
}
\]

Each layer below states:

- the mathematical object;
- the physical interpretation;
- the role in consciousness science;
- the strongest legitimate inference;
- the primary scholarly lineage.

---

# 1. Physical state and dynamics

A general controlled stochastic physical system may be represented as

\[
\boxed{
dX_t
=
f(X_t,u_t)\,dt
+G(X_t,u_t)\,dW_t,
}
\]

or by a transition kernel

\[
\boxed{
X_{t+\Delta t}
\sim
K_{\Delta t}(\cdot\mid X_t,u_t).
}
\]

The physical theory must specify the state variables, admissible interventions, observables, noise model, timescale, and boundary conditions.

## Consciousness-science role

Dynamics provide the substrate on which every later claim is built. Recurrence, stability, criticality, synchronization, broadcasting, integration, and causal influence are all properties of dynamics or distributions generated by dynamics.

A dynamical equation is not itself a consciousness equation. The bridge problem begins only after a physical invariant or equivalence class has been identified.

# 2. Information theory

For random variables \(X,Y\), mutual information is

\[
\boxed{
I(X;Y)
=
D_{\mathrm{KL}}
\left(P_{XY}\,\|\,P_XP_Y\right).
}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
I(X;Y)
=
H(X)+H(Y)-H(X,Y).
\]

This quantifies statistical dependence. It does not, by itself, identify causal direction or experiential structure.

## Primary lineage

- C. E. Shannon, **A Mathematical Theory of Communication**, *Bell System Technical Journal* 27 (1948), 379-423 and 623-656. DOI: `10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x` and `10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb00917.x`.
- T. M. Cover and J. A. Thomas, **Elements of Information Theory**, 2nd ed., Wiley, 2006. DOI: `10.1002/047174882X`.

## Research use here

Information theory supplies distances, dependence measures, coding limits, and decomposition tools. It is used as mathematical infrastructure, not as a bridge premise by default.

---

# 3. Information geometry

A parametric probability family

\[
p(x\mid\theta)
\]

can be represented as a statistical manifold with Fisher metric

\[
\boxed{
g_{ij}(\theta)
=
\mathbb E_{\theta}
\left[
\partial_i\log p(X\mid\theta)
\partial_j\log p(X\mid\theta)
\right].
}
\]

The infinitesimal statistical distance is

\[
\boxed{
ds^2
=
g_{ij}(\theta)d\theta^i d\theta^j.
}
\]

## Primary lineage

Shun-ichi Amari, **Information Geometry and Its Applications**, Springer, 2016. DOI: `10.1007/978-4-431-55978-8`.

## Consciousness-science role

Information geometry provides a principled way to compare families of physical probability laws and may eventually support representation-independent geometry of experimentally accessible physical states.

It does not determine the experiential metric. A bridge theorem would have to explain why a particular physical geometry corresponds to a particular experiential geometry.

# 4. Causal intervention and controlled response

For physical system \(p\), intervention \(u\), and delay \(\tau\), this repository uses

\[
\boxed{
P_p^{u,\tau}
=
\mathcal L(Y_{t+\tau}\mid do(u),p).
}
\]

A basic response distance is

\[
\boxed{
d_p^\tau(u,v)
=
\left\|P_p^{u,\tau}-P_p^{v,\tau}\right\|_{\mathrm{TV}}.
}
\]

The intervention notation distinguishes controlled causal manipulation from passive association.

## Primary lineage

- Judea Pearl, **Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference**, 2nd ed., Cambridge University Press, 2009.
- Jonas Peters, Stefan Bauer, and Niklas Pfister, **Causal Models for Dynamical Systems**, arXiv:2001.06208.

## Repository development

Propositions 11-17 use controlled response laws to build response geometry, directed influence, partition irreducibility, temporal continuation, composition, coupling, and coarse-graining results.

---

# 5. Neural complexity, integration, and segregation

An influential precursor to later integration theories is the neural-complexity program of Tononi, Sporns, and Edelman, which formalized the coexistence of segregation and integration in neural systems.

## Primary lineage

G. Tononi, O. Sporns, and G. M. Edelman, **A measure for brain complexity: relating functional segregation and integration in the nervous system**, *PNAS* 91(11) (1994): 5033-5037. DOI: `10.1073/pnas.91.11.5033`.

Giulio Tononi, **An information integration theory of consciousness**, *BMC Neuroscience* 5 (2004): 42. DOI: `10.1186/1471-2202-5-42`.

Larissa Albantakis et al., **Integrated information theory (IIT) 4.0**, *PLOS Computational Biology* 19(10) (2023): e1011465. DOI: `10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465`.

## Research discipline

No generic symbol \(\Phi\) should be used without specifying which theory/version, substrate definition, partition rule, cause-effect construction, and normalization are intended.

The repository therefore treats IIT as a serious formal bridge family, not as a generic claim that "more integration equals more consciousness."

---

# 6. Synergy and integrated information decomposition

Modern partial-information and integrated-information decompositions distinguish redundant, unique, and synergistic informational contributions.

A schematic decomposition is

\[
\boxed{
I(X_1,X_2;Y)
=
I_{\mathrm{red}}
+I_{\mathrm{unq},1}
+I_{\mathrm{unq},2}
+I_{\mathrm{syn}}.
}
\]

Andrea I. Luppi et al. used Integrated Information Decomposition to identify a synergistic workspace whose integration decreases with loss of consciousness under anesthesia or disorders of consciousness and returns with recovery.

## Primary source

A. I. Luppi et al., **A synergistic workspace for human consciousness revealed by Integrated Information Decomposition**, *eLife* 12 (2024): RP88173. DOI: `10.7554/eLife.88173`.

## Role here

This is strong empirical motivation for studying differentiated integration, synergy, and broadcasting. It does not establish that any one synergy statistic is a complete bridge signature.

---

# 7. Perturbational complexity and direct causal probing

Perturbational approaches are important because they test the system rather than only observing spontaneous activity.

Casali et al. developed the perturbational complexity index using cortical perturbation and distributed EEG response to discriminate a range of conscious and unconscious conditions.

## Primary source

A. G. Casali et al., **A theoretically based index of consciousness independent of sensory processing and behavior**, *Science Translational Medicine* 5(198) (2013): 198ra105. DOI: `10.1126/scitranslmed.3006294`.

## Role here

This evidence motivates controlled perturbation-response mathematics. The repository does not identify its full physical candidate with PCI.

---

# 8. Criticality and dynamical regimes

Critical dynamics are studied because systems near transitions can display long-range correlations, large susceptibility, and broad response repertoires.

A generic susceptibility is

\[
\boxed{
\chi
=
\frac{\partial \langle O\rangle}{\partial h},
}
\]

where \(h\) is an external perturbation and \(O\) an observable.

Maschke et al. reported relationships between spontaneous EEG critical dynamics, anesthesia-induced loss of consciousness, and perturbational complexity.

## Primary source

C. Maschke et al., **Critical dynamics in spontaneous EEG predict anesthetic-induced loss of consciousness and perturbational complexity**, *Communications Biology* 7 (2024): 946. DOI: `10.1038/s42003-024-06613-8`.

## Interpretation

Criticality is a dynamical regime, not an experiential identity. It belongs in the candidate-physics and falsification layers.

---

# 9. Free energy, prediction, and active inference

Variational free energy is commonly written

\[
\boxed{
F[q]
=
\mathbb E_q
\left[
\log q(z)-\log p(y,z)
\right].
}
\]

It bounds negative log evidence and supports a broad theoretical program linking perception, learning, and action to approximate inference.

## Primary source

Karl Friston, **The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?**, *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* 11 (2010): 127-138. DOI: `10.1038/nrn2787`.

## Consciousness-science role

The free-energy principle is a theory of adaptive biological organization and inference, not by itself a theory of consciousness. Predictive-processing and active-inference consciousness proposals require additional bridge assumptions.

---

# 10. Thermodynamics of information

Landauer's principle connects logically irreversible information processing to physical dissipation. In the idealized isothermal setting, erasure of one bit has minimum work scale

\[
\boxed{
W_{\mathrm{erase}}
\ge
k_BT\ln 2.
}
\]

## Primary source

Rolf Landauer, **Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process**, *IBM Journal of Research and Development* 5(3) (1961): 183-191. DOI: `10.1147/rd.53.0183`.

For nonequilibrium systems, stochastic thermodynamics tracks work, heat, and entropy production along fluctuating trajectories. A schematic second-law form is

\[
\boxed{
\langle\Sigma\rangle
\ge 0.
}
\]

## Primary source

Udo Seifert, **Stochastic thermodynamics, fluctuation theorems and molecular machines**, *Reports on Progress in Physics* 75 (2012): 126001. DOI: `10.1088/0034-4885/75/12/126001`.

## Role here

Thermodynamic constraints tell us what physical information processing costs and what nonequilibrium organization requires. They do not establish which thermodynamic property, if any, is sufficient for experience.

# 11. Quantum and factorization questions

Max Tegmark's state-of-matter program asked whether information, integration, independence, dynamics, and utility principles could distinguish observer-like physical organization and explored the associated factorization problem.

## Primary source

Max Tegmark, **Consciousness as a State of Matter**, *Chaos, Solitons & Fractals* 76 (2015): 238-270. DOI: `10.1016/j.chaos.2015.03.014`, arXiv:1401.1219.

## Role here

The conceptual contribution is important because it places the observer/factorization problem inside mathematical physics. The present repository separates that question from the additional physical-to-experiential bridge.

Quantum-mechanical language is not treated as evidence for a quantum theory of consciousness unless a concrete model, experimentally discriminating prediction, and empirical support are supplied.

# 12. Mathematical consciousness models

A bridge theory needs an explicit mathematical representation of both physical and experiential domains.

The repository formalizes the target as

\[
\boxed{
\bar B:\mathcal Q_P\to\mathcal Q_E.
}
\]

## Primary lineage

- Johannes Kleiner, **Mathematical Models of Consciousness**, arXiv:1907.03223.
- Johannes Kleiner and Sean Tull, **The Mathematical Structure of Integrated Information Theory**, arXiv:2002.07655.

These works are important because they treat the formal representation problem itself as part of consciousness science.

# 13. Theory discrimination

Competing consciousness theories should be compared through observable probability laws rather than through verbal similarity.

For theories \(\mathfrak T_1,\mathfrak T_2\), this repository defines

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_\Pi
=
\sup_{\pi\in\Pi}
\left\|
P_1^{\pi,q}-P_2^{\pi,q}
\right\|_{\mathrm{TV}}.
}
\]

The contemporary adversarial-collaboration literature makes this requirement especially important.

## Primary empirical source

Cogitate Consortium et al., **Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness**, *Nature* 642 (2025): 133-142. DOI: `10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1`.

# 14. Spaceflight and extreme environments

![Spaceflight and extreme-environment relevance](figures/spaceflight_extreme_environment_map.svg)

Spaceflight is included as an **application and stress-test domain** because exploration missions perturb many variables that affect cognition and operational state: sleep, circadian timing, isolation, radiation, altered gravity, workload, and sensorimotor function.

NASA's Human Research Program explicitly studies behavioral health and performance, including sleep, cognitive function, team performance, human-system interaction, and exploration risk.

Relevant authoritative sources include:

- NASA Human Research Program: `https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/`
- NASA Human Factors and Behavioral Performance: `https://www.nasa.gov/reference/about-human-factors-and-behavioral-performance/`
- NASA Behavioral Health Risk: `https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/esdmd/hhp/behavioral-health-risk/`
- NASA Sleep / Circadian Risk: `https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/esdmd/hhp/risk-of-performance-decrements-and-adverse-health-outcomes-resulting-from-sleep-loss-circadian-desynchronization-and-work-overload/`
- NASA ARCHeR Artemis Research for Crew Health & Readiness: `https://www.nasa.gov/reference/archer/`
- NASA NTRS, **Review of Human Cognitive Performance in Spaceflight**, Document ID 20120017374.
- NASA NTRS, **Risk of Adverse Cognitive or Behavioral Changes and Psychiatric Disorders...**, Document ID 20250001454.

## Legitimate connection to this repository

Spaceflight can test robustness of physical and cognitive markers under extreme perturbation. It does not provide direct evidence for a particular physical-to-experiential bridge.

# 15. Equation-to-evidence discipline

![Equation-to-evidence map](figures/equation_evidence_map.svg)

The repository applies the following rule:

\[
\boxed{
\text{equation}
\neq
\text{physical interpretation}
\neq
\text{measurement model}
\neq
\text{empirical evidence}
\neq
\text{bridge conclusion}.
}
\]

Every major result should therefore answer five questions:

1. **What is the equation?**
2. **What physical object does it describe?**
3. **How is that object measured?**
4. **What evidence supports the measurement-model relation?**
5. **What additional premise would be required to infer an experiential property?**

This separation is the central standard for the repository as it develops toward a comprehensive mathematical and physical reference for consciousness research.
