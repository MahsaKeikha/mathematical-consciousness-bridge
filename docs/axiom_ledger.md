## Axiom discipline

Each candidate axiom must eventually receive one of four labels:

- retained as a mathematical coherence condition;
- retained as an empirically motivated bridge hypothesis;
- weakened or replaced;
- rejected.

A theorem may depend on an axiom before that axiom is empirically validated, but the theorem must retain the dependency explicitly.
