# Fundamental Theory to Consciousness: A Falsifiable Mathematical Program

## Scientific status

There is currently **no experimentally established Theory of Everything** that unifies quantum theory, the Standard Model, and gravity. Quantum gravity remains an open research problem. This document therefore uses **fundamental theory** to mean a declared candidate microscopic theory together with its state space, dynamics, symmetries, observables, interventions, and empirical domain of validity.

The goal is not to assume that consciousness is quantum, gravitational, computational, holographic, or fundamental. The goal is to ask a precise question:

> If a candidate fundamental physical description were complete, what mathematical evidence would be required to show that experiential structure is, or is not, determined by that description?

This question extends the repository's existing quotient, identifiability, finite-data, intervention, temporal, composition, and scale framework to candidate fundamental physics.

## 1. What can legitimately be taken from *My Big TOE*?

Thomas W. Campbell's *My Big TOE* proposes that consciousness is fundamental and that physical reality behaves as a virtual reality generated within a larger information system. These claims are **not established scientific facts** and are not treated as physical premises in this repository.

A narrower part of Campbell's work is scientifically relevant as a **speculative falsifiable proposal**: Campbell, Owhadi, Sauvageau, and Watkinson proposed wave/particle experiments intended to test a finite-resource simulation hypothesis. The proposal begins from an explicit computational assumption about a simulator and derives experimental ideas from it. That makes it appropriate to discuss in a hypothesis-and-falsification section, but it does not establish the broader ontology of *My Big TOE*.

Repository classification:

| Claim or work | Status used here |
| --- | --- |
| Consciousness is the fundamental substance of reality | speculative metaphysical hypothesis |
| Physical reality is a virtual reality generated by consciousness | speculative model, not established physics |
| Campbell et al. simulation-test experiments | falsifiable speculative proposal |
| Standard quantum mechanics, general relativity, quantum information results | established or mainstream theoretical physics within stated domains |
| A complete physical-to-experiential bridge | open theorem and experiment problem |

Reference: T. Campbell, H. Owhadi, J. Sauvageau, and D. Watkinson, "On Testing the Simulation Theory," *International Journal of Quantum Foundations* 3 (2017) 78-99; arXiv:1703.00058.

## 2. Scientific foundations for a fundamental-theory interface

The following lines of work are included because they provide rigorous mathematical structure relevant to a future unification layer without making consciousness claims.

### 2.1 Quantum information and laboratory tests of gravity

Marletto and Vedral review information-theoretic methods for testing whether gravity can mediate quantum information. This is useful because it converts questions about quantum gravity into operationally defined experimental tasks rather than relying only on inaccessible Planck-scale models.

Reference: C. Marletto and V. Vedral, "Quantum-information methods for quantum gravity laboratory-based tests," *Reviews of Modern Physics* 97, 015006 (2025). DOI: 10.1103/RevModPhys.97.015006.

### 2.2 Entanglement and emergent spacetime

Several rigorous results and models connect quantum entanglement structure to spacetime geometry in restricted settings:

- T. Jacobson, "Entanglement Equilibrium and the Einstein Equation," *Physical Review Letters* 116, 201101 (2016). DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.116.201101.
- T. Takayanagi, "Emergent Holographic Spacetime from Quantum Information," *Physical Review Letters* 134, 240001 (2025). DOI: 10.1103/pg4r-fy8n.
- F. Pastawski, B. Yoshida, D. Harlow, and J. Preskill, "Holographic quantum error-correcting codes: toy models for the bulk/boundary correspondence," *Journal of High Energy Physics* 2015, 149 (2015). DOI: 10.1007/JHEP06(2015)149.

These results motivate a scientifically conservative idea: **spacetime itself may be an emergent representation of deeper relational or quantum-information structure in some theories**. They do not imply that consciousness is that structure.

### 2.3 Mathematical consciousness science

The experiential side must be formalized independently rather than defined to match the physics.

- J. Kleiner, "Mathematical Models of Consciousness," *Entropy* 22, 609 (2020). DOI: 10.3390/e22060609.
- M. Oizumi, L. Albantakis, and G. Tononi, "From the Phenomenology to the Mechanisms of Consciousness: Integrated Information Theory 3.0," *PLOS Computational Biology* 10, e1003588 (2014). DOI: 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003588.
- A. I. Luppi et al., "A synergistic workspace for human consciousness revealed by Integrated Information Decomposition," *eLife* 12, RP88173 (2024). DOI: 10.7554/eLife.88173.
- Cogitate Consortium et al., "Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness," *Nature* (2025), DOI: 10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1.

These works provide examples of mathematical phenomenology, causal/information-theoretic structure, empirical state dependence, and adversarial theory testing. None is treated as a final theory of consciousness.

# 3. Fundamental Relational State hypothesis

Let

\[
\boxed{\Omega\in\mathcal M}
\]

be a state in the state space of a declared candidate fundamental theory. The primitive object \(\Omega\) is intentionally theory-neutral: depending on the theory, it could encode fields, quantum states, algebraic observables, causal relations, strings, spin networks, tensor-network data, or another well-defined mathematical object.

Define four maps from the same underlying state:

\[
\boxed{
G:\mathcal M\to\mathcal Q_G,
\qquad
Q:\mathcal M\to\mathcal Q_Q,
\qquad
C:\mathcal M\to\mathcal Q_C,
\qquad
E:\mathcal M\to\mathcal Q_E.
}
\]

Interpretation:

- \(G(\Omega)\): geometric/spacetime equivalence class;
- \(Q(\Omega)\): quantum-operational equivalence class;
- \(C(\Omega)\): intervention-resolved causal equivalence class;
- \(E(\Omega)\): independently formalized experiential equivalence class.

The physical descriptor is

\[
\boxed{
T(\Omega)=\bigl(G(\Omega),Q(\Omega),C(\Omega)\bigr).
}
\]

This allows two sharply different scientific possibilities.

## Hypothesis F: physical factorization

There exists a bridge

\[
\boxed{B_T:\operatorname{Im}(T)\to\mathcal Q_E}
\]

such that

\[
\boxed{E=B_T\circ T.}
\]

Then the complete declared physical description determines the experiential structure.

## Hypothesis D: common-source dual emergence

Geometry and experience may both be emergent quotients of \(\Omega\), without experience being a function of the chosen physical quotient:

\[
\Omega\longmapsto G(\Omega),
\qquad
\Omega\longmapsto E(\Omega).
\]

This is **not** a claim of dualism. It is an open factorization question. If the declared physical quotient is genuinely complete and the factorization fails, then the theory has found a structural mismatch that requires explanation.

# 4. Exact Fundamental-Theory Non-Reducibility Theorem Target

Define physical equivalence under the candidate fundamental theory by

\[
\boxed{
\Omega\sim_T\Omega'
\iff
T(\Omega)=T(\Omega').
}
\]

Suppose independently defined experiential structure satisfies

\[
E(\Omega)\ne E(\Omega').
\]

Then:

\[
\boxed{
\Omega\sim_T\Omega'
\ \text{and}\ 
E(\Omega)\ne E(\Omega')
\Longrightarrow
\nexists B_T\ \text{with}\ E=B_T\circ T
\ \text{on both states}.
}
\]

The proof is immediate by contradiction: equal arguments of a function cannot have unequal outputs.

The difficulty is empirical and scientific, not algebraic. One must certify that:

1. \(T\) is complete for the declared physical theory and experiment class;
2. the two systems are actually equivalent under \(T\) within finite error;
3. \(E\) is independently specified and identifiable;
4. the experiential difference is not generated by an omitted physical variable, measurement bias, report confound, scale mismatch, or hidden intervention.

Only after all four conditions are met does the no-factorization result become scientifically meaningful.

# 5. Differential residual: searching for an independent degree of freedom

Let \(\Psi_T\) be a smooth, locally complete coordinate representation of the candidate fundamental physical quotient, and let \(\Psi_E\) be independently defined experiential coordinates.

If

\[
\Psi_E=g\circ\Psi_T,
\]

then

\[
D\Psi_E=Dg\,D\Psi_T,
\]

so the joint Jacobian cannot gain rank beyond the physical Jacobian:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}D(\Psi_T,\Psi_E)
=\operatorname{rank}D\Psi_T.
}
\]

Define the **fundamental-theory transverse residual dimension**

\[
\boxed{
d_{\mathrm{TOE}}^{\perp}
=
\operatorname{rank}D(\Psi_T,\Psi_E)
-
\operatorname{rank}D\Psi_T.
}
\]

A certified result

\[
\boxed{d_{\mathrm{TOE}}^{\perp}>0}
\]

would rule out a local smooth factorization of the experiential coordinates through the declared physical coordinates.

Scientific interpretation must remain conservative: this would establish an **independent formal degree of freedom relative to the declared representation**. It would not by itself prove a new spatial dimension, a fifth spacetime coordinate, nonphysical substance, or failure of quantum mechanics.

# 6. Information-geometric bridge residual

For finite data, differential rank can be unstable. A second route is to define an operational physical distance \(d_T\) and an experiential distance \(d_E\).

For matched pairs \((i,j)\), search for the regime

\[
\boxed{
d_T(i,j)\le\varepsilon_T
\quad\text{while}\quad
d_E(i,j)\ge\Delta_E}
\]

with

\[
\Delta_E\gg\varepsilon_E.
\]

Define a robust mismatch score

\[
\boxed{
R_{E|T}
=
\inf_{g\in\mathcal G}
\sup_i
d_E\!\left(\Psi_E(i),g(\Psi_T(i))\right),
}
\]

where \(\mathcal G\) is a declared admissible bridge class. A positive lower confidence bound on \(R_{E|T}\) is evidence against that bridge class. Increasing \(\mathcal G\) makes the test harder and guards against rejecting physical reduction merely because the wrong functional form was chosen.

This connects directly to the repository's existing P5 feature-sufficiency logic, P7 recoverability, P8 finite-error recovery, P9 sample complexity, P15 temporal certification, and P18 scale sufficiency.

The proposed architecture is

```text
candidate fundamental state Ω
          |

          |                    |                    |                   |
          v                    v                    v                   v
   geometry/spacetime     quantum operational   causal/intervention   experiential
       quotient G            quotient Q           quotient C          quotient E
          |                    |                    |                   |
          +--------------------+--------------------+                   |
                               |                                        |
                               v                                        |
                     complete physical map T                           |
                               |                                        |
                               +-------------- bridge test -------------+
                                              |
                         factorization / residual / falsification
```

This is the key innovation: **do not assume that spacetime is the deepest layer and do not assume that consciousness is the deepest layer. Put both hypotheses downstream of a common formal state and let factorization, invariance, and experiment decide what is required.**

# 8. Falsifiable research sequence

## F1. Candidate-fundamental-theory interface

For every candidate theory, declare:

\[
\mathfrak F
=(\mathcal M,\mathcal D,\mathcal G,\mathfrak I,\mathcal O,\Pi),
\]

where \(\mathcal M\) is state space, \(\mathcal D\) dynamics, \(\mathcal G\) gauge/symmetry structure, \(\mathfrak I\) interventions, \(\mathcal O\) observables, and \(\Pi\) admissible experiments.

## F2. Emergent-geometry validation

Determine whether a candidate coarse or holographic map

\[
G:\mathcal M\to\mathcal Q_G
\]

reconstructs known spacetime observables within certified error. Entanglement/geometry and quantum-error-correction results provide restricted mathematical testbeds.

## F3. Operational causal extraction

From the same underlying state, compute or estimate the intervention-resolved response structure already formalized by P11-P18.

## F4. Independent experiential geometry

Construct \(\mathcal Q_E\) without using the candidate physical signature in its definition. Psychophysics, similarity judgments, structured reports, state transitions, and cross-subject invariants are possible data sources, but each requires its own identifiability analysis.

## F5. Matched-state search

Search for states that are arbitrarily close under the complete physical metric but separated under the experiential metric. This is the empirical route to an exact or finite-error non-factorization witness.

## F6. Adversarial theory comparison

Compete multiple candidate bridges and multiple candidate fundamental descriptions on preregistered experiments. A serious theory should make different predictions from its alternatives.

# 9. No-free-metaphysics rule

Any proposed extra primitive \(Z\) must satisfy at least one of the following:

1. change a measurable prediction;
2. improve compression or prediction under out-of-sample tests;
3. resolve a proven factorization failure;
4. produce a new falsifiable invariant;
5. be removable as gauge/reparameterization, in which case it is not a new physical degree of freedom.

A variable that changes no observation, no intervention response, no bridge prediction, and no invariant is not empirically identifiable and cannot be counted as a discovered component of reality.

# 10. What would count as progress?

The program should be judged by theorem and experiment milestones, not by how sweeping its language sounds.

Strong milestones would include:

- a common operational interface for several candidate quantum-gravity models;
- a theorem connecting emergent geometry to an intervention-resolved information structure in a nontrivial model;
- a finite-sample estimator for the transverse residual \(d_{\mathrm{TOE}}^{\perp}\) or robust mismatch \(R_{E|T}\);
- independently validated experiential coordinates;
- preregistered matched-state experiments;
- a constructive counterexample to an overcompressed physical bridge;
- or, equally importantly, evidence that a sufficiently rich physical quotient does determine the experiential coordinates within uncertainty.

The last possibility matters. A scientifically honest consciousness program must allow the data to show that no extra degree of freedom is needed.

# Scientific boundary

This program does **not** claim that:

- a Theory of Everything has been found;
- consciousness is fundamental;
- reality is a simulation;
- spacetime is definitely emergent from entanglement in our universe;
- quantum mechanics is incomplete;
- consciousness creates wave-function collapse;
- an experiential residual would be a fifth spatial dimension;
- or an information-theoretic correlation is identical to subjective experience.

The purpose is to convert those broad ideas into explicit mathematical alternatives with identifiable failure conditions.
