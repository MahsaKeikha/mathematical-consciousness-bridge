## Why this record exists

The P75 model family is not closed under arbitrary averaging of different time-specific P75 laws. Therefore a pooled empirical law can violate P92 sign coherence even when each time regime is individually generated by a valid P75 law.

This page records an exact interior counterexample in rational arithmetic.

## Consequence for P94

This proves a clean no-go statement:

> Finite-range dependence alone can be handled by a dependence-adjusted concentration radius, but arbitrary temporal drift in the marginal law cannot be handled by simply applying P94 to the pooled histogram.

Accordingly, P94 requires one common marginal law across observations. A future drift theorem must either test stability first, condition on regimes, or define a different population target and prove the corresponding model obligation for that target.
