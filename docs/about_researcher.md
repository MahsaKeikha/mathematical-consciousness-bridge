# About the researcher

Mahsa Keikha, PhD, is founder and CEO of Connected Care. Her long-standing curiosity about consciousness became a more explicit scientific question during her doctoral years while conducting research at TRIUMF and studying quantum mechanics, particle accelerators, measurement, and physical description in greater depth.

During that period she wrote and completed an unpublished book exploring consciousness, human experience, physics, and questions raised by quantum mechanics. The manuscript was not published because it continued to feel scientifically incomplete: it could articulate important conceptual questions, but it did not yet have a mathematical framework strong enough to distinguish testable hypotheses from interpretation, to formalize measurement and identifiability, or to specify what evidence could falsify a proposed bridge claim.

The Mathematical Consciousness Bridge is, in part, a return to those earlier questions with formal definitions, sufficiency tests, identifiability conditions, measurement-channel models, finite-data guarantees, counterexamples, and reproducible computation.

Keikha's current work at Connected Care extends the same measurement problem into human-centered research involving human biomarkers, physiological and behavioral signals, multimodal sensing, aging, neuroscience, cognitive change, and dementia. At both the foundational and applied levels, the recurring question is what an observable signal can legitimately establish about an underlying state that is richer than the measurement itself.

This biographical history explains the motivation for the research program. It is not evidence for any theorem or empirical claim. The scientific record remains grounded in explicit assumptions, derivations, implementations, tests, external sources, reproducibility, and eventual empirical evaluation.
