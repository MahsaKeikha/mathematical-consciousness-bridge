## Citation discipline

A theorem in this repository is cited as a repository result when the mathematical statement is introduced here.

External literature is cited only for the conceptual or mathematical role it supplies.

The distinction between:

\[
\text{definition},
\quad
\text{bridge axiom},
\quad
\text{physical model},
\quad
\text{theorem},
\quad
\text{empirical evidence}
\]

is maintained throughout the project.
